  Recovery Semantics (Viva-Ready Honest Answer):
  - Guarantee: At-Least-Once delivery via Kafka consumer offsets.
  - Offsets are saved in Kafka (__consumer_offsets): Stage 2 resumes from the last committed offset.
  - In-memory state is LOST on crash: open tumbling windows, DGIM bit-buckets,
    and reservoir samples are wiped to zero on process restart.
  - Effect on tumbling windows:
    * The active window at crash time loses all pre-crash data. Upon restart,
      it starts accumulating from zero, producing an INCOMPLETE / undercounted summary.
  - Effect on duplicate counting:
    * Uncommitted events (processed in the ~5s auto-commit window before crash) ARE re-delivered.
      These events get processed twice (events_processed, DGIM, and reservoir double-counting).
  - Why Bloom Filter DOES NOT protect Stage 2:
    * Bloom Filter is located in Stage 1 (taxi.raw -> taxi.clean).
    * When Stage 2 crashes and re-reads taxi.clean, those events are already written
      to taxi.clean with bloom_seen_before=False. Stage 2 has no Bloom filter of its own.
  - Conclusion: Offsets bach jaate hain, par in-memory state kho jata hai;
    isliye restart ke baad window adhoori aur uncommitted events duplicate ho sakte hain.
